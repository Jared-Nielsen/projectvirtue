1\. Introduction & Canon Philosophy

“Britannia Eternal” is the definitive lore and world reference for Ultima VII: Project Virtue. It exists to protect the soul of Richard Garriott’s creation while giving the community room to expand it forever.Core Rule:  
All official content and approved player-created content must feel like it could have existed in a lost Ultima VII expansion disk from 1992\. New lore may be added, but it may never contradict, diminish, or retcon the events of Ultima I–VII (including Forge of Virtue).The world is living and persistent — history continues the moment the player steps into Britannia in 1992 (in-game calendar). Player and community actions can influence future events, but the foundational truths remain immutable.

---

2\. Cosmology & Fundamental Truths

* The World: Britannia is the surviving landmass of the shattered world of Sosaria. It exists in a dimension connected to Earth through Lord British’s summons.  
* The Three Principles: Truth, Love, and Courage — the philosophical bedrock from which the Eight Virtues were derived by Lord British.  
* The Eight Virtues:

| Virtue | Principle Combination | Associated City | Shrine Location |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Honesty | Truth | Moonglow | North of Moonglow |
| Compassion | Love | Britain | North of Britain |
| Valor | Courage | Jhelom | South of Jhelom |
| Justice | Truth \+ Love | Yew | Deep forest near Yew |
| Sacrifice | Love \+ Courage | Minoc | Mountains near Minoc |
| Honor | Truth \+ Courage | Trinsic | Near Trinsic |
| Spirituality | All Three | Skara Brae | Island of Skara Brae |
| Humility | None (the root) | New Magincia | Ruins of Old Magincia |

* Magic: Powered by the alignment of the planets, the Ether, and reagents. Blackrock and the Guardian’s influence are the primary corrupting forces in the Black Gate era.  
* The Guardian: The red-skinned extradimensional entity introduced in Ultima VII remains the overarching threat. His influence is subtle, insidious, and ongoing.

3\. Geography & Key Locations (1992 Canon Map)

Major Settlements (fully realized at launch):

* Britain – Capital, seat of Lord British, Virtue of Compassion. Castle Britannia is the political heart.  
* Trinsic – City of Honor, paladin stronghold.  
* Jhelom – City of Valor, warrior culture.  
* Minoc – City of Sacrifice, mining and craftsmanship hub.  
* Moonglow – City of Honesty, mage academy center.  
* Yew – City of Justice, judicial and druidic center.  
* Skara Brae – City of Spirituality, mystical island ruins (partially restored).  
* New Magincia – City of Humility, rebuilt from the destroyed original.  
* Vesper, Cove, Paws, Buccaneer’s Den – Smaller towns with unique flavor and side quests.

Wilderness & Dungeons:  
The world includes the full original wilderness, the Serpent Spine mountains, the Great Sea, and classic dungeons (Deceit, Despise, Destard, etc.). At launch these remain exactly as in 1992; procedural generation will only expand outward from the established borders.Moongates & Travel:  
Blue moongates for intra-world travel; red for inter-world; black for dimensional; silver for time (rare and dangerous).

4\. Historical Timeline (Canon Lock)

* Pre-Britannia: Sosaria fractured by the events of Ultima III.  
* Age of Enlightenment (Ultima IV–VI): Lord British establishes the Virtues, summons the Avatar multiple times, defeats Mondain’s legacy and Exodus.  
* The Black Gate Era (Ultima VII – current game start): 1992 in-game. The Fellowship rises as a false self-help movement secretly serving the Guardian. Ritual murders plague the land. The Black Gate is the ultimate threat.

Post-Launch Live Service Timeline:  
Official patches and expansions will advance the calendar year by year, allowing the Guardian’s influence to evolve and new threats to emerge — always building on, never replacing, the 1992 foundation.

5\. Major Factions & Organizations (1992 Status)

* The Fellowship – Seemingly benevolent self-help group; actually the Guardian’s primary cult.  
* The Companions of the Avatar – Iolo, Shamino, Dupre, and other classic party members (canonically still active).  
* The Order of the Eight Virtues – Loose monastic network maintaining the shrines.  
* Guilds – Mages of Moonglow, Warriors of Jhelom, Bards of Britain, etc.  
* Player-Created Factions (post-launch): Allowed and encouraged, but must register with the in-game “High Court of Britannia” system for persistence.

---

6\. Key Canon Characters & Their 1992 Status

* Lord British – Immortal ruler, wise but sometimes distant.  
* The Avatar – Player character; embodiment of the Virtues; can be male or female.  
* Iolo, Shamino, Dupre – Core companions; fully voiced and schedule-driven.  
* Batlin – Leader of the Fellowship (major antagonist).  
* The Guardian – Primary villain; voice heard in dreams and through red gates.

All original NPCs from Ultima VII are present and have full daily schedules, dialogue trees, and Virtue-reactive responses.

7\. UGC & Player-Creation Canon Rules

Allowed (Green Light):

* New towns, dungeons, quests, NPCs, and minor factions set after 1992\.  
* New Virtue trials or interpretations that reinforce the original Eight Virtues.  
* Player housing and guild halls that fit the medieval fantasy aesthetic.

Forbidden (Red Light – Auto-Rejected by Moderation):

* Retconning the Guardian’s defeat or Lord British’s immortality.  
* Introducing firearms, modern technology, or anachronisms.  
* Replacing or killing canon characters without official story approval.  
* Undermining the moral philosophy of the Virtues (e.g., “Virtues are fake” as a core message).

Alternate Britannia Mode: Optional toggle for purely creative servers where canon rules are relaxed.

---

8\. Visual & Atmospheric Canon Guidelines

* Art Style: 1992 pixel-art aesthetic with modern lighting, shaders, and resolution support. Color palette locked to original tilesets.  
* Music & Sound: Richard Garriott-approved style — orchestral with medieval instruments, ambient nature sounds, and dynamic day/night tracks.  
* Tone: Moral complexity, dry humor, wonder, and occasional horror. Never grimdark; always hopeful through the Virtues.
