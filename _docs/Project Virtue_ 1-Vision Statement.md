Vision Statement: Ultima VII – Project VirtueWe propose Ultima VII: Project Virtue, a licensed official remake that honors Richard Garriott’s groundbreaking vision for immersive, simulation-driven RPGs while evolving it into a living, player-shaped world. This is not a nostalgic remaster or a genre-shifting reboot. It is a faithful recreation of Ultima VII: The Black Gate—preserving its iconic isometric perspective, full-screen interactive environment, real-time simulation gameplay, and unparalleled depth of object interaction—while infusing it with the community-powered longevity and boundless scope that have defined modern successes like Roblox, Unreal Engine ecosystems, Star Citizen, and No Man’s Sky.At its heart, Project Virtue remains true to the retro soul of the original: a mouse-driven, non-grid world where every barrel, book, and blade can be picked up, combined, or manipulated exactly as Lord British intended. Combat stays tactical and party-based, dialogue remains rich and choice-driven, and the Eight Virtues continue to guide moral gameplay without compromise. There will be no platformer mechanics, no forced third-person action, and no departure from the classic overhead isometric view that made Ultima VII feel like stepping into a living painting of Britannia. The game will ship with the original campaign fully intact, rebuilt in modern tools for stability, accessibility, and visual clarity while retaining its hand-crafted pixel-art aesthetic and atmospheric charm.Yet we draw deliberate inspiration from today’s most successful living games to ensure Ultima VII thrives for decades, not years:

* Roblox-style creativity: Robust, in-game creation tools will let players design and publish their own quests, dungeons, towns, and storylines within the official Britannia engine. A moderated marketplace will reward creators, turning the world into an ever-expanding canvas for the community—exactly the spirit of user-generated worlds that has made Roblox a cultural phenomenon.  
* Unreal Engine-class technology: Built on a modern, highly extensible engine (with Unreal Engine compatibility in mind for modders), the game will offer unprecedented modding support—custom spells, AI behaviors, economy rules, and even new Virtue systems—without breaking the core simulation. Players will be able to reshape Britannia’s rules as deeply as they once did with Ultima VII’s own engine hacks.  
* Star Citizen-level persistence and simulation: A seamless, persistent online world where thousands of Avatars can coexist, trade, form guilds, wage wars, or pursue the Virtues together. Player-driven economies, dynamic events, and server-side simulation will make every action matter across a shared Britannia—echoing the ambitious, detail-obsessed persistence that defines Star Citizen.  
* No Man’s Sky-scale discovery and evolution: Procedural generation will expand the map beyond the original boundaries into new, explorable regions of Britannia and beyond, while a live-service roadmap guarantees years of free content drops—new quests, threats from the Guardian, and Virtue trials—keeping the world fresh and surprising long after launch.

This is the Ultima Garriott always dreamed of: a virtual society where ethics, exploration, and emergent storytelling reign supreme, now empowered by the very tools and philosophies that have made games into platforms. Project Virtue invites Lord British himself to return as creative steward, ensuring the soul of the series remains intact while giving new generations of players—and creators—the keys to the kingdom.We believe this vision will not only revive one of gaming’s most beloved masterpieces but will cement Ultima as a cultural institution for the next thirty years. We are eager to present a full proposal, technical demo, and partnership framework to make Ultima VII: Project Virtue a reality under the official license.

Executive Summary

Ultima VII: Project Virtue is a licensed, official remake that faithfully resurrects the 1992 masterpiece while transforming it into a living, community-driven platform for the next thirty years. We preserve every core pillar that made Ultima VII revolutionary — its iconic isometric view, full-screen interactive simulation, real-time world, and the profound moral philosophy of the Eight Virtues — while intelligently integrating the proven strengths of Roblox (user-generated content), Unreal Engine ecosystems (deep moddability), Star Citizen (persistent multiplayer worlds), and No Man’s Sky (procedural discovery and live evolution). 1\. Project Overview & Creative Vision

Ultima VII: Project Virtue is a persistent, isometric, simulation-first RPG set in the fully realized world of Britannia. Players return as the Avatar in a living, breathing recreation of the original 1992 campaign. Every barrel can still be moved, every book read, every candle lit, and every NPC schedule still runs in real time. The game ships with the complete The Black Gate storyline intact, rebuilt from the ground up with modern tools while maintaining the exact pixel-art aesthetic, mouse-driven interface, and simulation depth that defined the original.Beyond the classic single-player campaign, Project Virtue becomes a platform:

* Community Creation Layer (Roblox-inspired) — Players build and publish quests, towns, dungeons, and entire storylines using official tools.  
* Persistent Multiplayer Realm (Star Citizen-inspired) — Thousands of Avatars coexist in a shared, always-online Britannia with player-driven economies, guild halls, and dynamic events.  
* Procedural Expansion (No Man’s Sky-inspired) — New regions, dungeons, and Virtue trials are generated and refined over years of live service.  
* Full Modding & Engine Extensibility (Unreal Engine-class) — Creators can modify rules, add new spells, alter economies, or create entirely new Virtue systems.

The game will launch on PC (with console stretch goals) and will be built to support decades of free content updates, exactly as Garriott’s original series evolved from Ultima I through VII.

---

2\. Explicit Fidelity to the Original Ultima VII

To honor the license and Richard Garriott’s vision, the following elements are non-negotiable and will be preserved in every build:

* Isometric Perspective — Locked top-down isometric view only. No first-person, no platformer camera, no 3D action controls.  
* Full-Screen Simulation — The entire screen is the game world. No floating HUDs in normal play. All interaction is contextual and direct.  
* Object Interaction Depth — Every item in the world remains fully physics-enabled and interactive (pick up, stack, combine, ignite, poison, etc.).  
* Real-Time World — NPC schedules, day/night cycle, weather, and environmental simulation run continuously.  
* Virtues System — The Eight Virtues remain the central moral and mechanical philosophy. Player actions have permanent, visible consequences on reputation and world state.  
* Narrative Integrity — The original The Black Gate story, characters, and themes (including social commentary) are recreated verbatim before any expansion content is added.  
* Control Scheme — Mouse-driven point-and-click with optional keyboard support, exactly as in 1992\.

What We Will Never Do:

* Turn the game into a 3D action-adventure or platformer.  
* Replace the isometric view with over-the-shoulder or first-person as default.  
* Simplify or remove the deep simulation systems for “modern accessibility.”  
* Compromise the philosophical depth of the Virtues.
